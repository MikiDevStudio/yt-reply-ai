Reply AI YouTube comment assistant

01what it is

Replies to your comments, in your own voice.

A Chrome extension that sits in YouTube's own comment toolbar. Press it on a comment, pick a tone, and it drafts a reply from a profile you wrote about your channel — then you edit it, or throw it away, and press Reply yourself.

02how it is paid for

You bring your own OpenRouter key and pay the models at cost — a few dollars lasts months, and the free models cost nothing at all. There is no subscription to us, no account, and no server in between: the key stays on your machine with whatever spending cap you set on it.

Fifty replies a day are free. What a paid tier might add is on the waitlist page, unbuilt, waiting for a vote.

Not in the Chrome Web Store yet — the listing is being prepared. The code is public in the meantime, and it can be loaded unpacked from the repository.
